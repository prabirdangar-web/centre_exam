On Duty Declaration — School-wise, Teacher-wise (all duty days)

Builds one ON DUTY DECLARATION for a teacher, listing every date they served. The Teacher list below is grouped by feeder school, then by teacher within that school — sourced from the "Teacher name(s)" typed against each feeder school in the Invigilator Entry Sheet (Invigilator Requisition tab) — instead of a separate form per date.

Participation Certificate — School-wise, Day-wise (tick teachers)

Pick a feeder school and an examination date. Every teacher of that school (from the Room-wise Invigilator Allotment roster) is listed below with a checkbox. Tick the teacher(s) who actually served on that date, then generate a single Participation Certificate for just the selected teachers.
Select a school to list its teachers.

Participation Certificate — School-wise (after examination is complete)

Once all examination days are over, generate one consolidated certificate per feeder school, addressed to the Head of Institution of that school, listing every teacher sent by that school and every date each of them served — sourced from the Invigilator Entry Sheet.

Room-wise Allotment of Invigilator — H.S. Examination, 2027 [Semester-III]

Type each invigilator's Room No. (R.N.) against the examination date. The Venue Supervisor Data Sheet picks Invigilator 1 / 2 for every room from this chart automatically (still editable on the sheet).

Data Sheet; date columns follow the 2027 [SEM-III] routine. A date on which no student is allotted at the selected venue (Progress Ledger / Daily Entry totals are zero) is treated as an off day and dropped from this chart and its print automatically. Names and schools are pre-loaded from the office roster.

Pulls the subjects actually allotted per the Progress Ledger for the venue selected above, for each date. Rows with nothing allotted yet, or edited manually afterward, are left as-is. Subject Code and Room are worked out automatically and are not directly editable.

Invigilator Entry Sheet — Actual Allocation from Feeder Schools
For each date that requires invigilators from other schools, pick the feeder schools sending teachers and enter how many each is sending. Use "+ Add School" to bring in as many feeder schools as needed for that date. The total is checked against the required count from the schedule grid above. Once saved here, each school's requisition letter shows only that school's own count for each date. Under each school, tick the teachers who are serving on that date (the school's roster teachers are listed as checkboxes; you can also type names comma-separated in the field below the count). Ticking is extra detail only — it does not change the Count or the allocation totals — but it drives the Participation Certificates (school-wise and teacher-wise) just below the sheet and the School-wise / Teacher-wise On Duty Declaration further down this tool.
Participation Certificates — generated from the ticked teachers above
Only teachers whose box is ticked are counted, across every date. School-wise: one certificate per feeder school, addressed to its Head of Institution, listing each ticked teacher with the dates served. Teacher-wise: one personal certificate per teacher listing the dates, days and subjects served. Each can be printed singly or as a batch (one A4 page per certificate). The lists below refresh as you tick.
